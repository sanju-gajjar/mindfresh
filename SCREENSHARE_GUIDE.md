# Screen Share with Audio - User Guide

## Overview
The mindfresh chat application now includes a powerful screen sharing feature that allows you to share your screen and any audio from that screen with your chat partner.

## How to Use Screen Share

### Starting Screen Share
1. Click the **"🖥️ Share Screen"** button in the header
2. Your browser will open a "Share this screen with others?" dialog
3. Select which screen/window you want to share:
   - **Entire Screen**: Share your whole monitor display
   - **Window**: Share a specific application window
   - **Tab**: Share a specific browser tab
4. Click **"Share"** to start
5. You'll see a preview of your screen in a popup window on the left side

### What Gets Shared
✓ **Screen Content**: Everything displayed on your selected screen/window
✓ **Audio**: Any audio playing on your screen (videos, music, web content)
✓ **Cursor**: Your mouse cursor is visible to the partner

### Examples of Audio That Gets Captured
- 🎬 Instagram Reels, TikTok videos
- 🎵 Spotify, YouTube Music
- 📹 Netflix, YouTube, any streaming service
- 🎙️ Podcasts, audio recordings
- 💬 Voice calls, video conferences
- 🎮 Game audio
- 📞 VoIP calls

### Stopping Screen Share
**Option 1**: Click the **"Stop Sharing"** button in the screen preview popup

**Option 2**: Click the **"🖥️ Share Screen"** button again in the header

**Option 3**: Click the browser's "Stop sharing" button

Your camera video will automatically restore (if it was enabled).

## Technical Specifications

### Supported Browsers
- ✅ Chrome (version 72+)
- ✅ Edge (version 79+)
- ✅ Firefox (version 66+)
- ✅ Opera (version 59+)
- ⚠️ Safari (13+, requires additional permissions)

### Audio Quality
- Audio from your screen is transmitted directly to your partner
- No additional microphone needed to share audio
- Works alongside regular chat voice calls (if enabled)

### Screen Resolution
- Full resolution of your selected screen/window
- Adapts to your partner's available bandwidth
- Shows cursor for better collaboration

## Tips for Best Experience

### Before Sharing
1. **Close sensitive information**: Make sure no private data is visible
2. **Mute notifications**: Disable browser notifications on the screen you'll share
3. **Test audio**: Check that audio is working on your screen
4. **Choose the right display**: If you have multiple monitors, select the correct one

### During Sharing
1. **Move naturally**: Scroll, click, and navigate as needed
2. **Use cursor**: Your cursor is visible - use it to point things out
3. **Speak clearly**: Audio from screen is heard, plus any talking from your mic (if enabled)
4. **Inform partner**: Let your partner know what they're about to see

### Audio Sharing Best Practices
- 🎵 **Music**: Full volume for the best audio quality
- 🎬 **Videos**: Test audio levels before sharing
- 📞 **Calls**: Be aware your voice will be heard
- 🎮 **Gaming**: Game audio will be captured

## Troubleshooting

### Screen Share Won't Start
**Problem**: Clicking the button does nothing
- **Solution**: Check browser permissions for screen sharing
- Go to browser Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen sharing
- Make sure the website is allowed to share screens

### No Audio Is Heard
**Problem**: Screen is visible but no audio from partner's shared content
- **Solution**: 
  1. Check if audio is playing on the shared screen
  2. Ensure browser volume is not muted
  3. Try sharing again with audio sources

### Partner Can't See Your Screen
**Problem**: You shared screen but partner doesn't see it
- **Solution**:
  1. Check notification - "You started sharing your screen" should appear in chat
  2. Ensure internet connection is stable
  3. Try stopping and starting share again

### Screen Sharing is Slow/Laggy
**Problem**: Screen updates lag or freeze
- **Solution**:
  1. Check internet connection speed
  2. Close other bandwidth-heavy apps
  3. Try sharing a single window instead of full screen
  4. Lower other display quality (disable high-res monitors if possible)

## Privacy & Security

✅ **Private**: Only your chat partner can see your shared screen
✅ **Control**: You can stop sharing at any time
⚠️ **Be Careful**: Make sure to hide sensitive information before sharing

- 💬 Text Chat: Continue sending messages while screen sharing
- 😊 Emoji: All emoji reactions work during screen share
- 📎 File Sharing: Send images while screen sharing

## Keyboard Shortcuts (Browser-dependent)
- **Alt+Tab**: Switch windows (will be visible to partner if full screen shared)
- **Windows Key**: Open Windows menu (visible if sharing screen)
- **Cmd+Tab**: Switch apps on Mac (visible to partner)
